Privacy Policy

Your data, your call.

Last updated: September 15, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address — to deliver your playbook and receipt.
  • Your intake answers — household size, home type, region, and budget tier — used only to personalize your playbook.
  • Payment details — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

That's it. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no analytics fingerprinting.

How we use it

Your email and intake answers are used for one purpose: generating and delivering your personalized playbook. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use your household details in marketing.

How long we keep it

Order records (email, intake answers, playbook) are kept so you can re-download your playbook from your private link. Email info@mehyar.us any time and we'll delete everything tied to your purchase.

AI processing

Your intake answers are sent to an AI model to generate your personalized content. Inputs are used only to produce your deliverable — never for model training and never shared beyond the generation step.
