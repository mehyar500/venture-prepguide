The safer-home list, in the order to buy it

PrepGuide sizes a full readiness plan from six quick answers about your household — how many people, what kind of home, your region, your budget. However big that plan gets, the Black Friday buys below follow the same order: protect the home first, then warmth, then power, then the digital layer.

  • Security: know what's happening at your doors whether you're home, at work, or away for the weekend.
  • Warmth: blankets per person and a safe way to warm one closed room if the heat goes out with the power.
  • Light and power: a lantern for the room, a flashlight per adult, and a charged way to keep your phone alive — your phone is your news, your map and your lifeline.
  • Water and food: one gallon of water per person per day for three days, and shelf-stable meals nobody has to cook.
  • Documents and logins: IDs, insurance papers and the passwords you'd need away from home, reachable from anywhere — not just in a folder by the door.

Security is the layer that works every single day, not just in an outage. A house that looks watched over is a house that gets left alone — and if a winter storm ever sends you to a hotel or a relative's place, it's the layer that lets you actually relax while you're gone.
