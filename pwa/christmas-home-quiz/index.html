The Christmas home-readiness walk-through

Do this walk-through once, a few days before guests arrive — it takes less time than wrapping the last presents, and it covers the five layers PrepGuide sizes in every household plan.

  • Heat: know how your heating behaves, keep blankets per person where you can reach them, and pick the one closed room you would warm if the furnace stopped — a small room with a door holds heat far longer than an open-plan space.
  • Power: charge every power bank and lantern now, not during the outage. Your phone is your news, your map and your lifeline — keep a fast charger by the bed and one in the car.
  • Security: check door and window locks, test any sensors or cameras, and make the house look lived-in if you'll be away — a watched home is a home that gets left alone.
  • Water and food: one gallon of water per person per day for three days, plus shelf-stable food nobody has to cook. Christmas already stocks the kitchen — just make sure some of it needs no oven.
  • Documents: IDs, insurance papers and the logins you'd need away from home, reachable from anywhere — not only in a folder that could be in the one room you can't get to.
